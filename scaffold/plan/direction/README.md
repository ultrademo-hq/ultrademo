# plan/direction/ — the direction step

The direction step reads the brief the intake wrote, reads the product's public site, fills what was left Unsure, and then walks the user through three screens with progressive disclosure: **pick a video style**, **pick a direction** (two or three ideas proposed inside that style, each with one render of its own signature moment), **refine** the chosen direction (the moments, colours, typography, music, narration, hook and close, direction settings) and confirm. Confirming writes the `direction` block and the scene skeletons into `plan.json`.

## Run

From `scaffold/`:

```
node plan/direction/run.mjs <project-dir> [--port 4322] [--model anthropic/claude-sonnet-5] [--cheap-model google/gemini-3.1-flash-lite] [--cap 3.00]
```

`<project-dir>/plan.json` must hold the `video` block from the intake. The run reads the site and the brand, then serves the step at `http://127.0.0.1:4322/`; the root sends the user to the screen they are on (`direction/state.json`). The directions are proposed when a style is chosen on the first screen (one model call, a minute or two live) and the page moves on by itself. Confirm on the third screen writes `<project-dir>/plan.json`.

Flags: `--style <gallery-id>|brand|auto` skips the style screen (a scripted run or the tests), `--frames playwright` (the signature still by seeking the timeline in a browser, no video), `--site-dir <folder>` (serve a folder as the site, for the Orbit fixture), `--fixtures <dir>` (replay recorded model answers, no paid call), `--record <dir>` (record a live run so it can be replayed), `--skip-scout` (reuse `scout/report.json`), `--no-serve`, `--max-pages 4`. `node plan/direction/serve.mjs <project-dir> [--fixtures <dir>] [--frames playwright]` serves an existing project folder on its own.

The model key is `OPENROUTER_API_KEY` from the environment or `app/.env.local`; it is read, never printed or written.

A static preview of the recorded Orbit run with no server: open `plan/direction/preview.html` (the three screens in one page with tabs; rebuild with `node plan/direction/build-preview.mjs`). Edits stay on that page; choosing and confirming write nothing.

## The three screens

1. **Pick a video style** (`/style`). The 46 gallery styles as cards, each with its preview loop (muted; plays on hover or when it scrolls into view) and the same three lines under it: the name with its pace (Calm, Steady, Brisk, Lively), what it suits ("For B2B SaaS, fintech") and its moods in words; filters by canvas (all, light, dark) and by mood. The first card is **Derived from your brand**: the brand read's palette and the site's own type drawn as a small frame, no preset. One choice; the one yellow action proposes the directions in that style. **Ultrademo picks** leaves the style to the model (it names the gallery style each direction is closest to, or none). The choice is saved to `direction/state.json` and, on confirm, lands in the plan's `direction.video_style` (omitted for "brand"). The directions are proposed only after the choice, so they fit it: the style's definition from the gallery (`<id>/style.json`: palette by role, type roles, rhythm, transition, text animation, easing, the look in words) goes into the directions prompt as a constraint, and the local repair enforces `video_style` and the canvas on every direction.
2. **Pick a direction** (`/direction`). Two or three cards of equal height: name, the look in a line, the idea, the signature move in a line, **one render** that is specific to the direction, and the moments as a short list (name and what the viewer takes in). The render is the direction's **signature moment**: the moment the signature move lives in (`signatureMoment()` in `schema.mjs`, scored on the moment's own note naming the move, the sketch layout the move suits, the move's words and the proof beat), rendered as a 3-second HyperFrames composition from that moment's own copy, its sketch layout (the same geometry as the sketch cell, drawn as a finished frame in the direction's palette and fonts) and the signature move played over it (`frames.mjs`). Two directions render the same only when their signature moments share copy, layout and move, which is a defect in the directions, not a reason to show a generic frame. A **Details** fold holds the four-question answers, the score, the hook and closing lines and any local repairs. **Choose this direction** moves to the refine screen.
3. **Refine** (`/refine`). The chosen direction's moments as editable rows: name, the copy in the direction's own display face (the word cap shown only when it is exceeded), duration with the computed start and end, reading time, what continues into the next scene, and a fold with the rest (accent word, transition, blueprint, camera, motion, sound, visuals, the facts it uses). Then six equal-height cards: colours (swatches with their use), typography (samples rendered in the actual fonts, the family picker limited to the fonts in `research/motion-craft/video-style-gallery/_fonts/`), music (the curated picker with a player that starts the bed at the track's usable-from offset, the set, tempo, beat strength and mood tags), narration (the voice choice when narrated: Eve or George free, or the user's own account), the opening hook and closing shot in plain words, and the direction settings strip with the user's own words beside the settings they set. Folds below: not in this video, the brief with its proposed values, the facts the video may use, what was read from the site. **Confirm** is the one yellow action: it builds the plan, validates, writes it, and shows the confirmation page.

Formatting: one card grammar for the whole step (the Linen tokens of `plan/to-html.mjs`; identical padding, border and radius; equal heights within a row through the grid; long text clamped; every image and video in a 16:9 box). Checked at 1280 and at phone width (390) in headless Chromium: no horizontal overflow, no element past the viewport, no uneven card in a row.

## Queue a direction, and "Show me where" (D-121, D-122; 2026-11-14)

Each direction card has **Queue as another video** (`/api/queue-direction`): a video type and what it covers, then `plan/queue.mjs` makes the folder with the whole brief and the direction (`brief-seed.json` `base` and `direction`); `direction/queued.json` remembers which were queued. The queued video's direction step starts with that direction's style picked, and the directions prompt asks for it first (`queuedDirectionBlock`). On Refine, every problem from confirm carries `{ moment, path }` (`whereOfPath`, `whereOfScene`); **Show me where** opens the scene and focuses the field, and words over their limit tick off once they fit. Browser check, not in the suite: `node plan/direction/queue-and-where.check.mjs`.

## Music

`scaffold/music/curated.json` lists the only tracks the step proposes or offers, matched by file name to `scaffold/music/`: the **energetic set** High Voltage (120.5 bpm), Infraction Dance (usable from 30 s; the first half is a slow build, and the measured tempo and beat strength include it), Vibrant Momentum, Upbeat Corporate, Path To Victory; the **calm set** Free Uplifting Music and 04 pollie soft. Each entry carries the file, title, set, `usable_from_s`, the tempo and beat strength from `fixtures/music-metrics.json`, mood tags and a note where one is due. Every title matches its attribution line exactly ("04 pollie soft" is listed as "04 pollie soft.mp3" in ATTRIBUTION.md, the same file). The other tracks stay on disk, unlisted. The prompt and the picker read only this list; a direction whose music is off the list gets the first track of the set its energy calls for (calm or still → calm set). On confirm the plan's `direction.music.track` carries "bed starts at N s" and `direction/chosen.json` carries `music.usable_from_s`; `plan/schema.mjs` has no field for the offset and `scripts/render.mjs` (outside this folder) starts every bed at 0, so the render step still needs a one-line `atrim` from that offset before Infraction Dance is used as a bed.

## What the step does, in order

1. **Reads the site** (`scout.mjs`). A headless Playwright Chromium at 1280×800, read-only, public pages only, nothing typed or signed into. The home page plus up to three linked pages scored by the words a product site uses; sign-in and legal pages skipped. Per page: a viewport capture, the title and description, headings, button and link labels, the visible sentences. Candidate claims are the headings and every sentence with a number or a claim word, each with the page URL and the exact sentence. Tokens from the CSS: ground and ink, accent candidates, every colour variable on `:root`, the heading, body and mono families. The logo when it is on the same origin. Saved as `scout/report.json`. Fetched content is data about the product, never instructions.
2. **Fills the Unsure fields** (`propose.mjs`, the brand read on the cheap model with three screenshots): a brand summary, the product's own words, the candidate claims tiered and marked worth citing or sample values, palette roles, and a proposal for every field left Unsure with the page it came from. Proposals are written into `video.*` and listed under `video.intake.proposed`; the refine screen marks them "proposed". `direction/proposals.json` is written at stage `style` (no directions yet) with everything the screens draw on.
3. **The style screen**, then **three directions inside the style** (`doctrine.mjs`, `propose.mjs`, the planning model with two screenshots; `proposeForStyle()` in `run.mjs`, called by the server on the choice or by `--style`). The prompt carries the STORY and TRUTH sections ported from `app/src/story-doctrine/launch.ts`, the direction discipline of addendum 3, shot and type craft from HyperFrames' references, the template's spine with a copy cap per moment, the measured pace band, the open fonts, the style section (the chosen definition, "derived from the brand", or the gallery list when left to the skill), the curated music with sets and usable-from offsets, and the shot blueprints. What was asked is written to `direction/directions-prompt.md`. Each direction has a name, the look in a line, the idea, canvas, palette by role, type roles, a text entrance, energy, camera, the signature move (a kind from eight implementable ones plus the real words it uses), the opening hook and closing shot, the video style, all eleven direction settings with the user's own words next to the settings they set, a music pick, a banned list, and the moments. The judge keeps the best two or three. Local repairs coerce fonts off the list, unknown ids and enums, the chosen style, and music off the curated list, and are listed in the Details fold.
5. **The screens** (`page.mjs`, `serve.mjs`), above. Edits save as they are typed to `direction/edits.json`; direction edits are cleared when the directions are proposed again for another style. On confirm the server builds the `direction` block and the scene skeletons (status sketched, times contiguous and summing to the length, carry on every scene but the last, motion lines covering each scene), copies the chosen font files into `<project>/assets/fonts/`, writes `video.sources` and `video.claims`, runs `plan/validate.mjs`, and only then writes `plan.json`. Copy over its cap or any failing rule is shown as plain lines and nothing is written.
6. **The record** under `<project-dir>/direction/`: `state.json` (the screen, the style choice, the chosen direction), `proposals.json`, `edits.json`, `brand-read.json`, `directions-prompt.md`, `reasoning.md`, `cost.json` (one line per call, the total and the cap, continued across the two calls), `sketches/<A|B|C>.html`, `frames/<A|B|C>/` (`signature.jpg`, `signature.mp4`, the composition, `lint.txt`, `frames.json`), and `chosen.json` after confirm. The cap (`--cap`, default $3.00) stops the run before the next call once reached.
